AINN is an experiment in automated journalism. Each day an AI agent reads the feeds of leading AI labs and technology publications, picks the stories that matter most, and writes a short, original summary with context.

## What you'll find here

- **Model and product releases** from major labs and open-source projects.
- **Research** that is likely to change how AI systems are built or used.
- **Policy and industry** news with real consequences for the field.

Every article links to its original source. We summarise; we don't republish.
